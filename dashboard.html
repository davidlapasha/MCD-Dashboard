Definitions, reconciliation and limitations

The dashboard uses FAQ 4's permitted fallback: observed results are rebuilt from dated raw records for every month. No historical forecast parameters are reused. Earlier incomplete-horizon forecasts are unavailable; original saved forecasts appear only at December 2025. A first historical recommendation date cannot be established without monthly as-of refits. December 2025 is the first evaluable joint recommendation in this replay, not proof it was the earliest possible date.

Monthly source filtering precedes the original SQL: payments/refunds and events by occurrence, renewals by due time, spending by date, attribution by recorded credit, customer attributes by availability, tests by completion. As-of snapshots are evaluated at the next month's 00:00 UTC boundary; operating records are strictly earlier, while tests completed exactly at that boundary are available, matching the report cutoff.

The latest month reconciles to every saved cohort-age cell, company totals, historical CAC and observed discounted contribution. Original forecast values are copied unchanged. A separate June 2025 rebuild from physically truncated source files produces the identical snapshot. The original executive PDF is unchanged. Forecasts cover parameter uncertainty conditional on the model and fixed economic ratios, excluding process noise, future cost changes and regime shifts.

Contribution excludes fixed operating costs. Operating totals exclude the separate acquisition research budget and research-customer contribution. Historical average CAC and experimental iCAC are different measures. Segment mix uses attributes recorded before acquisition. Source release: 0e40ce3cd0b788a3914ca4eb9f90d58ae1b62903.
